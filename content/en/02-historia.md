# Story

## Brief summary

- Ollie was the star of a 1980s cartoon. Her show was cancelled and she was left forgotten in the studio.
- The studio didn't close: its team moved on to other projects and the classics room was shut. Then came AI-generated cartoons, which produce content non-stop but without soul; since the quality is not the same, the studio wants to reopen the classics room, and the AIs want to erase everything old so they don't lose their place.
- The AIs are preparing The Replacement, a perfect, soulless copy of her, to replace her for good.
- Ollie wakes up in a dark set and decides to escape. In each set she recovers a tape with a memory of who she was.
- In the set of potential ideas she defeats The Replacement and goes back on the air in a renewed show with soul.

## Detailed outline

### Prologue: the last frame

- When the player presses Play, an old tape shows the last frame in which Ollie was on the air. The image freezes: it is her last frame, and that frame leads into level 0.
- She wakes up in a set with the lights off and understands that if nobody remembers her, she will be erased.

### Set 1: the dark set (tutorial)

- She learns to move through the shadows and to use the pen and the correction fluid, and finds out that the enemies always smile and erase with an eraser.
- Her tape shows the sketch in which she was drawn for the first time.

### Sets 2, 3 and 4 (in random order)

- Each set is a different room of the studio, and its tape tells a memory that stands on its own, so the random order doesn't break the story.
- Proposed memories: adventures from her own show in which she was drawn, for example acting as a cowgirl, a detective or an explorer.

### Set 5: the set of potential ideas

- The Replacement waits for her, a perfect and generic copy of herself.
- If she defeats it, she recovers the last tape: the first frame of her new show, drawn by hand.
