# Cutscenes

Short scenes that tell the story without spoken dialogue. The durations are a proposal.

| Cutscene | When it plays | What it tells | Duration | Format |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Rating and logo | When the game opens | A rating screen in the style of PlayStation 2 games, followed by the team logo | 5 to 8 s | Still image with a fade |
| Prologue: the last frame | When the player presses Play | An old tape shows the last frame in which Ollie was on the air; the image freezes and she wakes up in the classics room | 15 to 20 s | Unity Timeline |
| Memory | When she picks up each set's tape | A frame from one of her adventures (for example, acting as a cowgirl); a short animation if there is time | 3 to 6 s | Image or 2D animation |
| Ending | After defeating The Replacement | With all her memories, she is the most complete cartoon in the studio and they put her on the air live | 15 to 20 s | Unity Timeline |
| Game over | When she loses all 3 hearts or runs out of time | Ollie is erased and everything goes dark | 3 to 5 s | Animation and fade to black |
