# Audio

## Style

Sound reinforces the Soul vs. Algorithm pillar. Everything tied to Ollie sounds acoustic and played by hand, like the music of classic cartoons: big band, vaudeville piano, pizzicato double bass, xylophone and exaggerated foley. Everything tied to the AI sounds perfect and synthetic: quantized electronic loops and processed voices like Google Translate's.

The music is layered by danger: when calm, a sneaky tiptoe theme; when an enemy investigates, percussion comes in; during a chase, the brass and a faster tempo come in. This way the player knows how things are going without looking at the screen.

References: the orchestras of classic cartoons, the jazz of Cuphead and the sneaky music of the Pink Panther as a tone idea.

## Music

- **Main menu:** a slow [vaudeville march](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CGn41TzqAZc) with piano, bass and the hum of the light bulbs.
- **Set 1 (tutorial):** soft [sneaky pizzicato](https://pixabay.com/es/sound-effects/search/pizzicato%20sigiloso/), to learn without pressure.
- **Sets 2 to 4:** the same sneaky theme with a different lead instrument per set (clarinet, xylophone, muted trumpet), in calm, alert and chase layers.
- **Memory (when picking up a tape):** Ollie's theme on a music box, with the sound of an old projector.
- **The Replacement (set 5):** Ollie's theme played by cold, perfect synthesizers, which falls apart as she wins.
- **Ending and credits:** the full theme in big band.

## Sound effects

- **Movement:** footsteps when walking (soft), running (loud) and crouching (almost silent); a crunch when stepping on pencil shavings.
- **Weapons:** pen shot (ink plop), impact on wet paper, correction fluid hit, cap click when swapping weapons with Q.
- **Enemies:** a "?" whistle when investigating and an orchestra hit on "!" when chasing; eraser squeak when hitting; paper falling apart when defeated.
- **Cameras:** camera motor when turning and a studio alarm.
- **Hit and defeat:** splash and dripping while she bleeds ink; a heart breaking; Ollie being erased.
- **Collectibles:** inkwell (glug-glug), heart (little bell), tape (projector starting up).
- **Ambience:** hum of the empty studio, ventilation and light bulbs.
- **UI:** pen scratch on hover and on button clicks.

## Voices

No spoken dialogue. Ollie uses cartoon vocalizations (surprise, effort, short laugh). The enemies repeat a cheerful, synthetic laugh, always the same. The Replacement uses her vocalizations, but tuned and perfect.

Sources: recordings by the team and free-license libraries (Freesound, Kenney, Pixabay), with credit. If an AI-made voice or music track is used, it is declared.
