# Conveyance

How the player gets information. The general rule: anything hand-drawn (pencil lines, paper) belongs to the protagonist, and anything shiny and perfect belongs to the enemy.

## Visibility

In the shadows, the protagonist looks muted; under a spotlight, she gets a bright outline. An eye icon in the HUD opens wider the more visible she is.

## Enemy alert

A hand-drawn symbol appears above each enemy: nothing while it patrols, "?" when it investigates and "!" when it chases. Its vision cone is drawn on the floor with pencil lines.

## Noise

Each noise draws scribble waves on the floor, as big as its radius. This way the player learns how far running, stepping on shavings or shooting can be heard.

## Hit and ink trail

When she gets hit, a heart in the HUD breaks, the screen shakes for a moment and the protagonist splashes ink. While she bleeds, she moves slower and leaves drops of ink in her palette colors on the floor, which dry in about 6 seconds. When the trail dries, she stops bleeding; the HUD only shows the empty heart.

## Cameras

Cameras project an amber light cone that is visible on the floor. Before the alarm, the cone turns red for half a second to give the player time to react.

## Interactive objects and collectibles

The tape, the inkwells and the hearts float and glow with pencil lines; the tape also sounds like a projector when she gets close. The exit door shows an outline when she gets close. In set 1, hand-painted signs teach the controls without pausing the game.

## HUD

A dark container holds the hearts (3), the stealth eye, the tape counter (x/5), the inkwell (it empties or fills up, with no numbers) and the equipped weapon with its Q key. The set's remaining time still needs a spot. In set 5, The Replacement's health bar appears. Design: Laura.
