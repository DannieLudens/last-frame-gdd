# Audience, market and analytics

> [!nota] First version built from what the GDD already says. The target numbers are a team proposal and will be adjusted with the Alpha data.

## Target player

| Aspect | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Age | 10 and up (expected rating E10+) |
| Player type | Likes stealth and short games with personality; young adults interested in classic animation and in the conversation about AI and creativity |
| Where they play | PC with keyboard and mouse, in the browser on itch.io |
| Session length | 5 to 10 minutes per full run |
| Games they already play | Cuphead, Mark of the Ninja, Bendy and the Ink Machine |
| What they look for | A short, replayable run with a handmade cartoon style and a story that works without dialogue |

## Comparable games

| Game | Platform | Model | What it shares | How we differ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cuphead | PC and consoles | Paid | Handmade classic cartoon look | Stealth instead of shooting; runs that last minutes |
| Mark of the Ninja | PC and consoles | Paid | Readable stealth: light, shadow and noise on screen | 3D with a high-angle camera and an art-vs-AI theme |
| Bendy and the Ink Machine | PC and consoles | Paid | An animation studio and ink as the material of the world | For all ages, no horror |
| Epic Mickey | Consoles | Paid | Forgotten cartoons, ink and paint | A short indie game, in the browser |

## Analytics

| Source | What it measures | What decision it informs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| itch.io (creator dashboard) | Visits, browser plays, downloads and where players come from | Which promotion channel works |
| In-game events | In which set players lose and why (hearts or time), times detected, tapes collected and final grade | Which set is too hard and whether the time limit is fair |
| Tester form | Feedback by category: controls, stealth, art, sound and story | What to fix for the Beta |

In-game events to log (proposal): `set_start`, `set_complete`, `tape_found`, `player_detected`, `player_hit`, `game_over` (cause: hearts or time) and `run_complete` (time and grade from S to E).
