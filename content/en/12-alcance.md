# Project scope

## Development time

Development runs from November 24 to December 7, 2026, with a 9:00 to 17:00 workday, plus extra hours at night or at other times when someone can't work during the day because of personal activities, and the pitch to publishers on December 11. These are the bootcamp dates, unless the mentors change them:

- Sprint 1, core mechanics: due Thursday, November 26, 4:15 p.m.
- Sprint 2, Alpha on itch.io: due Thursday, December 3, 9:30 a.m.
- December 4: feature freeze. From then on, only bug fixes and polish.
- Sprint 3, Beta on itch.io: due Monday, December 7, 4:15 p.m. Final build and tests of the 6 possible room combinations.
- Game Connect: Friday, December 11, 9:15 a.m. Pitch to publishers.

## Length of a run

**Proposal:** between 5 and 10 minutes for a full run (5 rooms). The order of the 3 middle rooms is random, so the run can be replayed with a different route.

## Costs

The team isn't paid (it is a bootcamp project), but for the pitch we estimate how much it would cost to make the game in Colombia with a hired team, over 3 weeks (0.75 months). Exchange rate used: COP 3,309.55 per dollar ([official rate on November 18, 2026, Portafolio](https://www.portafolio.co/economia/finanzas/dolar-en-colombia-frena-su-racha-alcista-y-cae-este-miercoles-18-de-noviembre-este-es-su-precio-oficial-506559)).

### Team

- **Salaries:** 2 programmers (Sebas and Daniel) at **COP 2,812,680 per month**, the average salary of a junior developer in Colombia ([Indeed, September 2026](https://co.indeed.com/career/junior-developer/salaries)).
- **Salaries:** 3 art, UI and sound profiles (Oscar, Laura and Nerlyn) at **COP 2,500,000 per month**, the fee in a real junior graphic designer job posting (Computrabajo). We found no specific reference for junior sound design, so we used the same one.
- **Employer costs:** about 52% on top of the salary for benefits, social security and payroll taxes ([hacecuentas.com, Colombian benefits calculator](https://hacecuentas.com/co/calculadora-pcs-prestaciones-sociales-colombia-percent-salario)), plus the digital connectivity allowance of COP 249,095 per month for salaries up to 2 minimum wages (since the team works remotely, it replaces the transport allowance and has the same value) (2026 minimum wage: COP 1,750,905, Decree 1470 of 2025).
- **Team total:** COP 13,125,360 in salaries + 52% = COP 19,950,547, plus allowances of COP 1,245,475 = COP 21,196,022 per month. **For 3 weeks: COP 15,897,017**.

### Software and licenses (1 month)

- **Unity Personal:** free while revenue and funding stay under US$200,000 per year (https://unity.com/products/unity-personal).
- **Photoshop:** 2 single-app licenses at US$22.99 per month (https://www.adobe.com/creativecloud/pricing).
- **Figma:** 2 Professional seats at US$20 per month billed monthly (US$16 billed annually).
- **ChatGPT:** 1 Plus subscription at US$20 per month (https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6950777-what-is-chatgpt-plus).
- **Free:** Blender, GitHub, Trello, Miro and itch.io on their free plans: US$0.
- **Subtotal: US$105.98 = COP 350,746**.

### Unity Asset Store assets

- **Flat Kit:** US$39.90 at regular price (https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/vfx/shaders/flat-kit-toon-shading-and-water-143368).
- **Senses 3:** US$50 (https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/tools/behavior-ai/senses-3-ai-vision-hearing-smell-system-345466).
- **Outline:** included in Flat Kit, at no extra cost.
- **Subtotal: US$89.90 = COP 297,529**.

### Equipment and services (our own estimate)

- **Computers:** 5 computers of about COP 4,000,000 each, depreciated over 36 months: COP 111,111 per month each. For 3 weeks: COP 416,667.
- **Internet and power:** about COP 120,000 per person per month. For 3 weeks: COP 450,000.

### Total

- **Project subtotal:** COP 17,411,959.
- **Contingency:** 10%: COP 1,741,196.
- **Estimated production cost:** about **COP 19.2 million (around US$5,790)** for 3 weeks of production. So far, the team's real spending is the Asset Store packages and personal subscriptions.

## Platforms

PC with keyboard and mouse, playing in the browser (WebGL) on itch.io. Estimated minimum requirements, to be confirmed with the build: an up-to-date desktop browser with WebGL 2.0 (Chrome, Edge or Firefox), 4 GB of RAM (8 GB recommended), integrated graphics from the last 5 years and a 1280 × 720 screen or larger.
