# Characters

Character sheets. The traits come from the story and the art sketches; whatever is still open is marked.

## Ollie

- **Name:** Ollie.
- **Role:** playable protagonist.
- **Origin:** star of a 1980s cartoon who did everything in her show (adventurer, cowgirl, detective). After it was cancelled, she was left forgotten in the studio.
- **Personality:** brave, rebellious, clever and a bit nostalgic. She is at war with oblivion and with the decision to replace her with perfect but soulless animations.
- **Look:** classic cartoon with pencil lines, rounded shapes and a silhouette that reads well from above. Rebel-girl outfit: slate blue jacket (#687589), muted brick pants (#A65F45) and boots. Brown hair.
- **Abilities:** walk, run, crouch, ink pen (ranged), correction fluid (melee), weapon swap with Q. Health: 3 hearts.
- **Animations:** idle, walk, run, crouch, shoot ink, correction fluid hit, swap weapon, get hit and bleed ink, get erased, pick up tape, victory.

How Ollie evolved: the team voted between three sketches and two palettes, and chose sketch 3 with palette B.

## AI cartoons (enemies)

- **Role:** regular enemies. Number per set: at least 3, to be tuned during testing.
- **Concept:** generic characters like the ones seen today in ads made with AI. The current reference shows them in office and business clothes (progress and productivity), but the final design is still open.
- **Personality:** permanent smile, efficient, emotionless.
- **Look:** perfect lines, symmetry, bright saturated colors, as if printed on paper. The opposite of Ollie's pencil lines.
- **Behavior:** they patrol, investigate noises, follow the ink trail and chase with their eraser. They take 2 ink hits; from behind, the correction fluid erases them in one hit.
- **Animations:** patrol, investigate, chase, hit with eraser, get stained, dissolve.

## The Replacement (final boss)

- **Role:** boss of set 5.
- **Concept:** a copy of Ollie perfected with AI, but soulless, made to replace her for good. Oscar is drawing the sketch.
- **Look:** the same design as hers, but perfect, vector-like and shiny, with no hand-drawn lines.
- **Behavior:** phases and attacks to be designed in Sprint 2.
